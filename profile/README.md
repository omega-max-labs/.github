<!-- Description -->
<div style="flex: 2; min-width: 500px; text-align: center; font-family: 'Arial', sans-serif;">
  <h2 style="margin-bottom: 25px;">Description</h2>
  <p>
    Omega Max is a centralized, role-based management platform designed to unify the work of all operational teams within large digital communities. It serves as the core control system for staff coordination, automation, analytics, and decision-making across multiple functional departments.
  </p>
  <p>
  Unlike traditional moderation panels focused on a single role, Omega Max is built as a multi-team environment, where each staff group operates within its own dedicated workspace while remaining part of a shared ecosystem. All teams are integrated under one platform with consistent logic, permissions, and workflows.
  </p>
  <p>
  Supported Teams:
  
  - Support Team
  - Moderation Team
  - Creative Team
  - Closemod Team
  - Event Management Team
  - Tribune Moderation Team
  - Project Management Team

  </p>
  <p>
  Each team in Omega Max is provided with:
  
  - a dedicated dashboard with real-time statistics and updates
  - personal profiles and activity tracking
  - team management panels for hierarchy, status, and performance control
  - specialized operational panels tailored to their specific responsibilities
  </p>
  <p>
  Omega Max follows a modular and scalable architecture, allowing communities to enable only the teams they need while remaining ready to grow. All systems are designed with transparency, auditability, and long-term scalability in mind, making the platform suitable for both medium-sized communities and large, high-traffic projects.
  </p>
  <p>
    By centralizing staff workflows, operational logic, analytics, and automation into a single platform, Omega Max reduces fragmentation, eliminates manual overhead, and creates a structured, predictable environment for community management. This allows teams to focus on quality, coordination, and growth rather than operational chaos.
  </p>
</div>
